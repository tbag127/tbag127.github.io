# Super Forbidden Game - Game Mechanics and Level Design

## Core Game Mechanics

### Character Switching
- Players can switch between the three main characters: Hang Yi, Xin Na, and Ji Kairui
- Each character has a unique ability that is essential for solving puzzles
- Character switching is done via a simple UI button or keyboard shortcut
- Only one character can be active at a time

### Telekinesis (Hang Yi)
- **Activation**: Press and hold the action button while pointing at movable objects
- **Visual Effect**: Objects glow with a blue aura when they can be manipulated
- **Limitations**: Can only move objects within a certain range and weight limit
- **Puzzle Uses**:
  - Move blocks to create paths
  - Stack objects to reach higher areas
  - Hold down pressure plates
  - Remove obstacles blocking the path

### Time Manipulation (Xin Na)
- **Activation**: Press the action button to create a time-slow bubble around Xin Na
- **Visual Effect**: The screen gets a slight blue tint, and moving objects leave trails
- **Limitations**: Limited duration with a cooldown period
- **Puzzle Uses**:
  - Slow down moving platforms to time jumps better
  - Navigate through fast-moving hazards
  - Solve time-sensitive puzzles that would be too quick otherwise
  - See "ghost" images of future paths

### Elemental Control (Ji Kairui)
- **Activation**: Press the action button to toggle between fire and water elements
- **Visual Effect**: Character glows red (fire) or blue (water) based on the active element
- **Limitations**: Can only use one element at a time
- **Puzzle Uses**:
  - Fire: Light torches, melt ice obstacles, activate fire-based mechanisms
  - Water: Extinguish fires, create water bridges over gaps, activate water-based mechanisms

## Level Design

### Level 1: The Awakening
- **Setting**: A mysterious school campus after hours
- **Story**: Hang Yi discovers his telekinesis powers while trapped in the school
- **Objective**: Escape the locked school building using newly discovered powers
- **Gameplay Flow**:
  1. Tutorial section teaching basic movement and controls
  2. Introduction to Hang Yi's telekinesis power
  3. Simple puzzles involving moving objects to reach new areas
  4. Encounter with Xin Na, who helps with her time powers
  5. Final puzzle requiring both characters' abilities to unlock the exit

#### Level 1 Puzzles:
1. **Blocked Doorway**: Move desks and cabinets blocking a door
2. **Raised Platform**: Stack boxes to reach a high ledge
3. **Timed Security Gate**: Use Xin Na's time slow to pass through a gate that closes too quickly
4. **Combination Lock**: Use telekinesis to arrange symbols in the correct order while using time slow to beat a timer

### Level 2: The Challenge
- **Setting**: An ancient temple with elemental trials
- **Story**: Ji Kairui tests Hang Yi and Xin Na's mastery of their abilities
- **Objective**: Navigate through the temple's trials by combining all three characters' powers
- **Gameplay Flow**:
  1. Introduction to Ji Kairui and his elemental powers
  2. Series of rooms with increasingly complex puzzles
  3. Final chamber requiring precise coordination of all three abilities

#### Level 2 Puzzles:
1. **Fire and Ice**: Use Ji Kairui's fire to melt ice barriers, then Hang Yi's telekinesis to move the resulting water
2. **Time and Elements**: Use Xin Na's time slow to navigate through alternating fire/water jets, then use Ji Kairui to counter the elements
3. **Suspended Platforms**: Use telekinesis to position platforms, time slow to cross them safely, and elemental powers to activate mechanisms
4. **Final Trial**: A complex puzzle room where all three powers must be used in sequence - elemental switches, time-sensitive moving platforms, and telekinetically controlled objects

## Mobile Controls

### Touch Interface
- **Left Side**: Virtual joystick for movement
- **Right Side**: Action button for character-specific abilities
- **Top of Screen**: Character switching buttons with icons
- **Bottom of Screen**: Additional context-sensitive actions

### Responsive Design
- UI elements scale appropriately for different screen sizes
- Touch targets are large enough for comfortable interaction on mobile devices
- Visual feedback for all interactions (button presses, ability activations)

## Accessibility Features
- Adjustable difficulty for puzzle timing
- Visual cues to supplement audio cues
- Option to increase contrast for better visibility
- Customizable controls
