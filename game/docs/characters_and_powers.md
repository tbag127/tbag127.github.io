# Super Forbidden Game - Characters and Powers

## Main Characters

### Hang Yi
- **Role**: The protagonist, a young student who discovers his supernatural abilities
- **Personality**: Determined, curious, and protective of his friends
- **Supernatural Power**: Telekinesis - ability to move objects with his mind
- **Game Mechanic**: Players can move specific objects in the game environment to solve puzzles

### Xin Na
- **Role**: Hang Yi's close friend and ally
- **Personality**: Intelligent, cautious, and analytical
- **Supernatural Power**: Time manipulation - ability to slow down time briefly
- **Game Mechanic**: Players can activate a time-slow effect to navigate through fast-moving obstacles or solve time-sensitive puzzles

### Ji Kairui
- **Role**: A mysterious figure with knowledge of the supernatural world
- **Personality**: Enigmatic, wise, and sometimes unpredictable
- **Supernatural Power**: Elemental control - ability to manipulate fire and water
- **Game Mechanic**: Players can switch between fire and water abilities to overcome environmental obstacles

## Character Relationships
- Hang Yi and Xin Na are close friends who discover their powers together
- Ji Kairui serves as a mentor figure, guiding them to understand and control their abilities
- The three characters must work together, combining their unique powers to overcome challenges

## Supernatural Powers as Game Mechanics

### Telekinesis (Hang Yi)
- Move specific objects marked with a special glow
- Stack objects to reach higher areas
- Hold dangerous objects in place temporarily
- Pull distant items closer

### Time Manipulation (Xin Na)
- Slow down moving platforms or hazards
- Create a time bubble where puzzles can be solved at a slower pace
- Rewind small mistakes (limited use)
- See echoes of future paths

### Elemental Control (Ji Kairui)
- Fire: Light torches, melt ice obstacles
- Water: Extinguish fires, create bridges over gaps
- Switch between elements to solve environmental puzzles

## Game Levels Concept

### Level 1: The Awakening
- Setting: School campus where the characters first discover their powers
- Objective: Help Hang Yi learn to control his telekinesis to escape a locked building
- Puzzles: Simple object manipulation, moving platforms, and basic obstacle avoidance

### Level 2: The Challenge
- Setting: A mysterious temple where Ji Kairui tests the characters' abilities
- Objective: Combine all three characters' powers to navigate through the temple's trials
- Puzzles: Complex puzzles requiring switching between characters and combining their abilities
