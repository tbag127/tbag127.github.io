# M-Shape Descent Hold Design Documentation

## Design Overview
This climbing hold features an ergonomic M-shaped design optimized for safe descent movements. The design incorporates multiple grip positions while maintaining safety through rounded edges and appropriate depth profiles.

## Key Design Features

### Shape and Ergonomics
- M-shape provides three distinct contact points for secure grip
- Symmetrical design allows for both left and right-hand use
- Valley depth of 15mm creates secure thumb placement
- Side walls angled at 15° for comfortable grip positions

### Safety Features
- All edges rounded with 10mm radius to prevent finger injuries
- Textured surface for consistent grip in varying conditions
- Even load distribution across contact points
- No sharp corners or potential pinch points

### Technical Specifications
- Overall width: 140mm
- Height: 80mm
- Depth from wall: 45mm
- Bolt hole: 3/8" countersunk mounting
- Material thickness: Minimum 12mm around mounting point

### Material Properties
- High-grade polyurethane resin
- UV-resistant formulation
- Non-slip textured surface finish
- Color: Standard climbing hold colors (to be determined by manufacturer)

## Usage Guidelines
1. Primary Use: Descent routes and rest positions
2. Recommended placement: 
   - Vertical walls
   - Slight overhangs (up to 20 degrees)
3. Load Rating:
   - Designed for dynamic loads up to 2kN
   - Static load rating: 4kN

## Manufacturing Notes
- Single-piece mold design
- Integrated texture pattern in mold
- Quality control points:
  - Edge radius verification
  - Texture consistency
  - Bolt hole alignment
  - Material cure verification
