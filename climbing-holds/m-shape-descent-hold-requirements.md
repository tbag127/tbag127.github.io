# M-Shape Descent Hold Design Requirements

## Safety Requirements
1. Positive Edge Profile
   - Minimum edge depth of 20mm for secure grip
   - Rounded edges to prevent finger injuries
   - No sharp corners or points

2. Material Considerations
   - High-quality polyurethane resin
   - UV-resistant material
   - Non-slip textured surface

## Dimensional Specifications
1. Overall Size
   - Width: 140mm (to accommodate the M shape)
   - Height: 80mm
   - Depth from wall: 45mm

2. M-Shape Design
   - Symmetrical M pattern
   - Valley depth: 15mm (for secure thumb placement)
   - Peak radius: 10mm (to prevent sharp edges)
   - Side walls angled at 15° for ergonomic grip

## Mounting Specifications
1. Bolt Pattern
   - Single 3/8" bolt mounting point
   - Countersunk design
   - Minimum material thickness around bolt hole: 12mm

## Ergonomic Considerations
1. Grip Positions
   - Multiple grip options for different hand sizes
   - Thumb catch in center valley
   - Rounded profile for comfortable descent moves

2. Load Distribution
   - Even pressure distribution across the palm
   - No pressure points on fingers during descent
   - Support for both straight-arm and bent-arm positions

## Usage Context
- Primary use: Descent routes
- Secondary use: Rest positions
- Suitable for both indoor and outdoor climbing walls
